Welcome to Minesweeper

Instructions

Press a cell to reveal it. The number on it indicates how many bombs are nearby
that cell veticaly, horizontaly, and diagonaly.

💣 In singleplayer, discovering a bomb loses the game
To win the game, discover all cells without the bombs

🚩 Right-click the cell to flag it if you believe there is a bomb
You can not reveal the bomb this way and you will avoit it.
(SMALL TIP) if you have that many flags that sais a number, you can click it
and reveal all it's neighbours, even bombs if the flag is placed incorrectly.

The game menu is on top of the board

010 🙂 000

On the left it is the mine counter, on the right is the timer,
and on the middle is the 'New Game' button.

On the bottom of the board are the map size options
There are presetted small(9x9 with 10 bombs),medium(16x16 with 40 bombs)
and large(16x30 with 99 bombs).
Customize button creates a map exacly how you like.
Adjust the map size and mine numbers from the inputs.

To enter multiplayer mode, press right click on the 'New Game' button
On the left is RED players' score, on the right GREEN players' score.
In the middle, the 'NewGame' button shows who's turn it is by it's color.
+2 point for clicking on a safe cell
+5 points for flagging a bomb
+0 for flagging a wrong cell and it will be revealed
-10 points for clicking a bomb cell

I hope you enjoy the game! Have fun!
